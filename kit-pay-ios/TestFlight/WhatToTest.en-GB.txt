Chat scrolling is fixed. On a long conversation the timeline used to ignore a
finger completely - the drag did nothing at all - because every render folded
the whole message history on the main thread. The fold is now computed once per
state change, so scrolling, flicking and dragging in a 2,000-message thread
answer immediately. Please open your longest chats, tap "Jump to latest
message", and drag straight away.

Sharing is steadier. Sharing a photo, video, file or link into Kit Pay from
another app keeps a background assertion while it writes to the shared
container, so iOS no longer ends the share the moment the sheet is put aside.
Please share from Photos, Files and Safari, and try switching away mid-share.

Everything else should be unchanged. Please report anything that is not.
