Build 106 answers the four items of the 21 September device report.

1. Sharing into Kit Pay works again. On build 105 a signed-in phone could not
share anything: the sheet ended in "Nothing was sent. Sharing is locked or your
account changed." Sharing no longer depends on Face ID or on the app being
unlocked - biometric verification is for payments only - and a phone left in
that refused state heals on the first launch of this build, with nothing to
reset. Please share a photo, a video, a file and a link into Kit Pay from
Photos, Files and Safari, including straight after a cold launch without
unlocking anything, and while switching away mid-share.

2. Scrolling a chat full of media. Photo, video, voice-note and document rows
no longer decode anything while they are being drawn, and every bubble now asks
for the size it actually shows instead of up to twelve times that. Please open
your busiest thread and flick hard in both directions, over photos, voice notes
and documents, then scroll back over rows you have already passed.

3. Swiping left and right through media. Tap a photo in a chat - a single one,
one inside a multi-photo message, or one that is still uploading - and swipe.
Every photo and video in that conversation is now in the same gallery, in the
order they were sent. Please swipe through several, rotate the phone, pinch to
zoom and close.

4. Verifying with Face ID. On the pay screen and on the home screen Kit Pay now
asks once each time you come back to the app, not repeatedly, and what is
underneath stays blurred until it succeeds. Cancel the prompt: the screen must
stay blurred, with a Retry button, and nothing should flash into view before or
after.

Everything else should be unchanged. Please report anything that is not.
